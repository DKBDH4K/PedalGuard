From College Workshop to Your Peace of Mind.

This is the story of how a simple, mechanical idea became a trusted solution for vehicle security.

The Beginning

A Problem We Knew Firsthand

Our journey didn't begin in a corporate boardroom; it began in the innovation labs of Xavier's Institute of Engineering, Mahim. As engineering students, we were constantly challenged to find practical solutions to real-world problems. Living and commuting in a city like Mumbai, the persistent threat of vehicle theft was a problem we knew all too well.

The Idea

Strength in Simplicity

We noticed that while security technology was getting more complex with alarms and trackers, it also created new vulnerabilities that clever thieves could exploit. We believed the most powerful solution would be one that was elegantly simple, purely mechanical, and undeniably effective. It's a straightforward idea backed by robust, intelligent design.

The Prototype

Engineering a Solution

What started as a Third-year project, fueled by late-night study sessions and a passion for hands-on engineering, quickly evolved into the first Pedalguard prototype. The principle was clear: if a thief can't use the pedals, they can't steal the vehicle.

Our Mission: Engineering Your Peace of Mind

Our mission is to bring that same engineering-driven solution from our college workshop directly to you. We are dedicated to providing every vehicle owner with an affordable, powerful, and incredibly reliable anti-theft device.
